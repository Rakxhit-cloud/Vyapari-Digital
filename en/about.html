About Us

Live Khaata is a Hindi-first digital ledger app built for GST-registered shopkeepers, especially traders who buy stock from suppliers on credit.

Our aim is to make supplier dues, rate-wise GST and the reports needed for GST available directly on the phone, without any accounting knowledge. Records are built automatically from bill photos and voice entries.

What we offer

A licence to use the Live Khaata app, purchased on this website with a one-time payment. The app is available through the Google Play Store.
