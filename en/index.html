Features

Supplier ledger

Outstanding amount and age of every bill. Payments by cash, UPI, NEFT or cheque are recorded with their reference numbers, including part-payments against a single bill.

Bill-wise dues with ageing and payment references.

Bill scan

One photo of a bill fills in the supplier name, GSTIN, bill number, date, total and rate-wise GST, even on bills with more than one tax rate.

One photo reads the whole bill, with rate-wise GST.

Rate-wise GST

CGST + SGST or IGST is determined automatically from both parties' GSTINs. If a rate can't be read from the photo, it can be filled in with a single tap.

CGST + SGST or IGST, decided automatically.

GST reports

Purchase register in GSTR-2B order, ITC summary.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any charges beyond ₹799 + GST?

No. It's a one-time payment: ₹799 plus 18% GST, ₹943 in total. No renewals, AMC or annual fees. Enter your GSTIN at checkout and the invoice will carry it, so you can claim ₹144 as input tax credit.

Can I pay the agent in cash?

No. Our agents do not accept cash. They send a payment link or QR, so the money goes directly to Live Khaata's bank account and the GST invoice is emailed to you. If anyone asks for cash, please inform us by email.

Is there a limit on bill scans?

Up to 200 scans a day, well above what a typical shop needs. Voice entries and supplier ledgers are unlimited.

Will data import directly into Tally?

Not at present. You get a voucher list with debits and credits, from which your CA or accountant can make the entries.

What if there's no internet?

Entries are recorded offline and saved automatically once you're connected. Only bill scanning needs internet.

Can the app run on two phones?

Yes. You can log in on two phones with the same number, and both stay in sync.

How secure is my data?

Your accounts are protected by a PIN and backed up to the cloud. We never sell your data or share it for advertising or marketing.

Will this file my GST return?

No. It is an organised record of your purchases, ready to hand to your accountant. Reconcile with GSTR-2B before claiming ITC.
