What information we handle

Why we handle it

  • To run the app for you — storing your entries so you can view, edit, and access them on any device after logging in.

  • To keep the service secure — preventing fraud and abuse.

We do not use your business records for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the app to you.

Where your data is stored

Your entries are stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, which acts as our infrastructure provider on our behalf. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by security rules so that only your own logged-in account can read your data — other users cannot access it.

Do we share your data?

We do not sell your data and we do not share your business records with third parties for their own purposes. The only processors involved are Google's services (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, reCAPTCHA), which process data solely to operate the app on our behalf. We may disclose information only if required by law.
