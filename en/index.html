Questions shopkeepers ask

What happens after the 15-day trial?

You choose Lifetime or a Google Play plan. If you don't, the app stays open in view-only mode: all your records, statements and downloads stay available, and new entries need a plan.

Are there any charges beyond ₹799 + GST?

No. Lifetime is one payment of ₹943 including GST. No renewals, AMC or annual fees.

I'm on a Google Play plan. Can I switch to Lifetime?

Yes. Buy Lifetime here with the same mobile number, then cancel your plan in the Play Store. Your records stay as they are.

Can I pay an agent in cash?

No. Agents send a payment link or QR, so the money goes straight to Live Khaata and the GST invoice is emailed to you. If anyone asks for cash, tell us by email.

Is there a limit on bill scans?

30 bill scans a day on every plan. Voice entries, typed entries and supplier khatas are unlimited.

Where is my data kept?

On Google Cloud, linked to your account. The app opens only with your PIN or fingerprint, and our admin tools can't read your entries.
