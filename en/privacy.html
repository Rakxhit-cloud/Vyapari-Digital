Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Live Khaata is a sole proprietorship (GSTIN 09IASPK2450N1ZW) based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh. This policy explains what information the Live Khaata app and website collect, how it is used, how long it is kept, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (used for OTP login), your name, and the Google account you link (name and email address only — used to recover your account and send invoices; we never read your emails). Your shop or firm name, address, state and GSTIN are optional.
  • Business records you enter: entries of money received and paid, supplier details, bills, payments, notes and related records.
  • Bill photos you scan, and the details read from them.
  • Voice entries: spoken entries are usually converted to text on your phone. Only when the phone cannot understand the amount is the short clip sent to our server (Google Cloud AI) to interpret that one entry. The clip is not stored — only the amount and note are saved in your entry.
  • Contacts: when you add a supplier, only the one contact you pick (name and number) is saved. We never read your contact list.
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you scan a bill or make a voice entry, and only with your permission.
  • Technical information: basic app usage events (such as when the app was opened and which feature was used), error records, last active time and a notification token, used to operate the service, fix problems and keep it secure. These do not include what you say or the notes in your entries.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Razorpay or Google Play. We receive the payment status and reference, never your card, UPI or bank credentials.

How we use it

To provide the service and keep your records backed up and in sync; to read bills and voice entries; to activate your licence and issue GST invoices; to provide support; to prevent fraud and misuse; and to meet legal and tax obligations.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed on Google Firebase and Google Cloud. Servers may be located outside India, including in the United States. Bill photos and voice entries are processed by Google Cloud AI services solely to extract the information you asked for.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising or marketing. We share information only with service providers who help us run Live Khaata (Google Firebase and Google Cloud, Razorpay, SMS services for OTPs and email services for invoices), and with authorities where required by law.

Security

Access is protected by OTP login and an app PIN, and data is encrypted in transit. Each account can access only its own records.

Retention and deletion

How long we keep data:

  • Your account and entries: while your account is active.
  • Basic app usage records: up to 12 months, for security and fraud prevention, then deleted.
  • Tax (GST) invoices: for the period GST law requires.
  • Voice clips: not stored.

Delete your whole account: in the app, tap More → Delete account → confirm → enter the code sent to your number. Your account, all entries, purchase bills, suppliers, bill photos and shop details are deleted from our servers immediately.

Delete your data but keep your account: More → Erase all data → enter the code. All entries, purchase bills, suppliers and bill photos are erased; your login and shop details stay.

Without the app: email support@livekhaata.app with the subject “Delete my Live Khaata account” and your registered mobile number. We delete it within 7 days of confirming it is you. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, and raise a grievance. Contact us using the details below.

Children

Live Khaata is intended for businesses and is not meant for use by anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be notified in the app or on this website.

Grievance Officer

Email: support@livekhaata.app. The Grievance Officer's details are on the Contact Us page. Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month.
